Robo-Greeno · Data A · Stage A learning lab — forward & inverse kinematics of one 3-DOF leg

One leg, three joints

Every spider leg has three powered joints. The coxa swings the whole leg left and right (yaw). The femur and tibia lift and fold it in a vertical plane. Inverse kinematics (IK) is the question this lab answers: given a point in space where you want the foot, what three joint angles put it there? Drag the sliders below and watch the leg solve itself.

The math, with your numbers

The solve splits in two: the coxa from the top view, then the femur and tibia as a flat 2-link arm. No iteration — it is exact, closed-form trigonometry the students can derive and check by hand.

The same solve, in code

This is the starter for Stage A — one function. In MuJoCo it runs once per leg; each leg passes its own target and the shared geometry.
