# 📖 The Physical AI Tutorial

A four-chapter, beginner-friendly tour of **Physical AI** — taught through Robo-Greeno, a six-legged agricultural spider robot. Every concept gets a physical metaphor before any math. Each chapter is ~15 minutes.

> [!TIP]
> This page renders two ways, both fully clickable: as a styled site on **[GitHub Pages](https://ingyukoh.github.io/robo-greeno-data-a-1/)**, and natively **right here on GitHub**. No build step needed to read it.

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | What you'll learn |
|---|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | **[LLM AI vs Physical AI](01-llm-vs-physical-ai.md)** | What changes when intelligence gets a body — tokens vs torques, Moravec's paradox, why robots need simulators. |
| 2 | **[Legs and Fingers — Nature's Engineering](02-legs-and-fingers.md)** | Six legs, eight legs, two legs, ten fingers: why each design exists, and why a hexapod is the best first robot. |
| 3 | **[MuJoCo Without Tears](03-mujoco-intuition.md)** | How a physics simulator actually works — bodies, joints, actuators, timesteps, contact forces — with zero hand-waving. |
| 4 | **[Reinforcement Learning, Intuitively](04-rl-intuition.md)** | States, actions, rewards, PPO, and sim-to-real, all through one hexapod learning to walk. |
